The Ox: mockups, version 4

The fix: every service now has its own identity color that never changes, and status (green, orange, red) is kept separate. Identity tells you WHICH service; status tells you HOW USED. The identity colors below were picked so none of them can be confused with green, orange or red. All numbers are percent used, sample data only.

Claude: purple, circle ChatGPT: white, square Grok: cyan, hexagon Grok Bot: pink, diamond

1. Taskbar: level lines

Thin level lines like an audio meter. Top line is the 5 hour bucket, bottom line is the weekly allowance, number at the end of each. Grok and Grok Bot only have a weekly allowance, so they get one line. Colors are toned down: the service name is a soft tint, the line is a muted green, amber or red by percent used.
